CORAL ISLAND

Coral Island, also known as Koh Larn, is a popular day-trip destination near Pattaya, Thailand, known for its clear turquoise waters and vibrant coral reefs. Visitors can enjoy activities like snorkeling, parasailing, and relaxing on pristine beaches. The island features several beachfront restaurants serving fresh seafood and local cuisine. It’s a popular spot for snorkeling, parasailing, and other water sports. Visitors can enjoy fresh seafood at beachfront restaurants and explore vibrant coral reefs teeming with marine life.
