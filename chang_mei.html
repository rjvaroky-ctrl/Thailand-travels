CHIANG MEI

Chiang Mai, known as the "Rose of the North," is home to over 300 ancient temples, including the famous Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, perched on a mountain with stunning city views. The city hosts the world-famous Yi Peng Lantern Festival, where thousands of glowing lanterns are released into the sky, creating a magical spectacle. Chiang Mai is a paradise for nature lovers, offering lush mountains, elephant sanctuaries, and one of the most scenic hiking trails in Thailand.
